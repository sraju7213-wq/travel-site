## Pexels Package Photography (replaces brochure crops)

Package thumbnails, package detail galleries, destination cards, experience cards,
pillar panels and navigation imagery are now sourced from Pexels
(https://www.pexels.com/) under the Pexels License and served from the Pexels
CDN (`images.pexels.com`) with
`auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&w=1200&h=800&dpr=2`.

**The canonical, complete source reference — every photo ID, its Pexels source
page, its CDN URL and the component that renders it — lives in
[`docs/image-sources.md`](../../../../docs/image-sources.md).** That file is the
single place to check or update provenance.

Page-level hero banners (About, Contact, Houseboats, Journeys and the category
pages) were deliberately left on their original local brochure crops; they are
listed in §9a of `docs/image-sources.md`.

## Brand logo (`app/public/logo.png`)

The brand lockup is a supplied client asset — the Taj Mahal, a temple, the
Himalayan ridge, a flight path and the "INDIA NEPAL / TRAVEL CENTER" wordmark,
drawn in white on a transparent background for the site's dark theme.

| File | Source | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `app/public/logo.png` | Client-supplied artwork (`India Nepal Travel Center Logo.png`) | Re-encoded to 1200x400 (3:1) with an indexed alpha palette — 43 KB, down from 563 KB at 2172x724. Visually identical at 3x zoom. The wordmark is baked into the artwork, so it is not duplicated as HTML text in `components/BrandMark.tsx`. |

Because the wordmark now lives in the image itself, the separate
`India Nepal` / `Travel Center` text block that previously sat beside the logo
was removed from `BrandMark`. The link keeps its `aria-label`, so the
accessible name is unchanged.

## Notes

- Crop rectangles are expressed in an 848x1200 review space and scaled to the
  2379x3366 source raster at build time.
- `gateway-of-india` is published in the brochure as a low-opacity card background;
  it receives a contrast/saturation correction so the monument stays legible.
- `manifest.json` holds the same table in machine-readable form.
