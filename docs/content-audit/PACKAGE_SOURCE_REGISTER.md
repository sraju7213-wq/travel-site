## How to read this register

- **Printed name/code** — exactly as it appears in the brochure
- **Brochure page** — printed page number (not PDF viewer offset)
- **Source duration/grade** — as printed on the card
- **Website duration** — what the website currently shows
- **Status** — `source-supported`, `needs-confirmation`, or `withheld`
- **Notes** — known issues, conflicts, or flags
